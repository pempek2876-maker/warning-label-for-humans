Entry for the make.fun competition **Warning Label for Humans**
(https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85):
the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come printed on
every human being, 160 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> WARNING: Will agree to plans while meaning to cancel them later. All commitments void upon contact with a sofa.

## How it was chosen

`main.ts` holds 12 candidate warning labels, each scored on the judge's
published criteria in the judge's order: a real warning label for humans,
the laugh landing in one read with the turn in the last few words, truth
about people in general, the wording and rhythm of a real printed label,
and using the line with no filler. Every candidate meets every other in a
full round-robin (66 pairings); the winner took all 11 of its pairings —
everyone recognizes the plan agreed to and quietly cancelled, and the
label's own legal phrasing ("void upon contact") delivers the turn. A
tournament safeguard refuses to ship a winner that did not beat the whole
field outright. The program is deterministic (two runs, byte-identical
output) and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it locally with
`mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
